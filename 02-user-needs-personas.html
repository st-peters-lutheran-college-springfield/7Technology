User Needs & Wants

Good design starts with a real user. Analyse all three teacher personas, then choose who you're designing for.

Learning intention

You can analyse all three teacher personas and justify, with evidence, which one your final CyberPet design will suit.

Explicit teaching

This is the Empathise and Define stage of the Human-Centred Design process (see Phase 5). Good design starts with understanding a real user's needs, preferences and context — not just what looks good to you.

You must design for one of the three teachers below — but you must analyse all three before choosing, and justify your choice in your folio.

Mrs Wong

Year 7 English Teacher, 32

  • Soft greens & pastel tones
  • Pilates, journalling, reading
  • Calm, organised, reflective

Design feel: gentle, calm, uncluttered

Mr Brolly

Year 7 Science Teacher, 28

  • Bright blues & neon accents
  • Touch football, STEM clubs
  • Energetic, playful

Design feel: fun, bold, dynamic

Mrs Nora

Pastoral Care Teacher, 54

  • Warm neutrals & purples
  • Gardening, knitting, community work
  • Warm, supportive, safe

Design feel: comforting, safe, caring

Linked resources

  • Read What is Human-Centred Design? — see the Empathise and Define stages in particular.

Activity

For each of the three personas, note: what colour palette would suit them, what form/shape would feel right on their desk, and how their classroom vibe should show up in your mood-display pattern (gentle pulsing vs. bright flashing, for example).

Then choose one persona and justify your choice.
