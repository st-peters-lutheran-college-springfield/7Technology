Design Elements & Principles

The building blocks designers use, and the human-centred process that ties this whole unit together.

Learning intention

You can apply elements and principles of design to explain how form supports use, and why your shape suits your user.

Explicit teaching

Elements of design

The "ingredients" you combine:

  • Form — the overall 3D shape of your CyberPet
  • Shape — the 2D outlines and profiles that build that form
  • Colour — sets the mood of your design
  • Texture — how a surface looks or feels

Principles of design

The "rules" for arranging elements well:

  • Balance — visual weight distributed sensibly
  • Contrast — differences that create interest or clarity
  • Emphasis — drawing attention to the important part (e.g. the light display)
  • Unity — everything feels like it belongs together

Activity

Pick two elements (e.g. form and colour) and two principles (e.g. emphasis and balance). For each, explain how you'll use it in your CyberPet design, and why it suits your chosen persona.

Then write one or two sentences on how your form will support your CyberPet's use (housing electronics, sitting securely, showing light clearly).
